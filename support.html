Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my high score?

Open the game, go to Settings, and tap Reset Highscore (tap twice to confirm). This only affects your local device.

How does the leaderboard work?

Only your single best score is shown — submitting a new run only updates the leaderboard if it beats your previous best. You can change your displayed leaderboard name any time in Settings.

I found a bug or the game crashed — what should I include in my report?

  • What you were doing right before it happened
  • Your device model and OS version
  • A screenshot, if possible

How do I change which side the controls are on?

Settings → Controls lets you switch the on-screen D-pad between the left and right side of the screen.
