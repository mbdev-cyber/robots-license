Privacy Policy for Robots

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Marcel Bax ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game Robots (the "App").

Summary

  • The App has no account, login, or sign-up — we don't collect your name, email, or any contact details.
  • Your score and app settings (theme, control layout) are stored only on your own device and never sent to us or anyone else.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob. Google (and its ad partners) may collect device identifiers and other data to serve and measure ads, as described below.
  • We do not operate our own servers and do not otherwise collect, store, or sell personal information ourselves.

Information Collected Through Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob (part of Google's Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertising, including interstitial ads. To do this, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (e.g. Apple's IDFA, Android's Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate (and, if permitted, precise) location
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • App interaction data related to ads (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google and its partners under their own privacy policies, not by us directly:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from partner sites/apps

On first launch, users in the European Economic Area, the UK, and other applicable regions are shown a consent screen (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before any personalized advertising takes place. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy Settings inside the App.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require or request your name, email address, phone number, or any other directly identifying information. The App does not include chat, user accounts, or any feature that would let us collect user-generated content.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your high score and preferences (theme, which side the on-screen controls are on) are saved in the App's local storage on your device only. This data is never transmitted to us and is deleted if you uninstall the App.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

For Users in the European Economic Area & UK (GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA or UK, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) / UK GDPR applies. Where processing depends on your consent (such as personalized advertising), the legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); certain basic ad delivery, fraud prevention, and security processing may instead rely on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

You have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Request data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time (via Settings → Ad Privacy Settings in the App), without affecting processing already carried out
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Because most of the data described above is collected directly by Google as an independent controller, requests about that specific data (e.g. advertising identifiers) may need to be directed to Google as well as to us.

For California & Other US State Residents (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws)

Depending on your state, you may have the right to know what personal information is collected about you, to request deletion of that information, to correct inaccurate information, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral (interest-based) advertising — which can include the kind of ad identifier sharing described above.

You can opt out of personalized/interest-based advertising in the App at any time via Settings → Ad Privacy Settings. We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not otherwise sell or share personal information outside of the advertising functionality described in this policy.

International Data Transfers

Google and its advertising partners may process data in the United States or other countries. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) as described in Google's own privacy documentation linked above.
